JAMMU & KASHMIR CAPABILITY ATLAS · Grow Bros · v3.1 showcase · H3 res-9 coordinate-queryable · unified binding over the Kashmir Valley atlas + Kashmir & Jammu flora catalogues
Cell elevation is modelled — an inverse-distance-weighted surface from 55 anchor points plus a regional terrain skeleton; exact at anchors, a smooth approximation between. The production upgrade is a real DEM (Copernicus/SRTM 30 m) doing zonal-stats per H3 cell in PostGIS. Prices are indicative wholesale/mandi/export-benchmark ranges (₹), volatile by grade & season — planning numbers, not quotes; Jammu USD benchmarks converted at ₹84/$. CITES / endangered species: the legal path is cultivation & certified extract under NMPB / Forest-Department frameworks, never wild trade. Warm-climate crops stay confined to Jammu's low zones and alpine roots to the highland belts — the vertical stacking is enforced by the elevation logic.
